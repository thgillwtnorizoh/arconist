D / F / J / K = four ground lanes from left to right.
Cyan: tap and release at the green line. Amber: retain the key. Green: core-confirmed
hold activity. Checkpoints drive feedback/counters. Short release is not an instant
miss; overdue unsupported checkpoints become LOST. No hit sounds are included.

without fresh tap arbitration. Ordinary clicking still supports taps.
