ARCONIST R004 — ordinary taps and holds / human qualification build

Extract the ENTIRE ZIP to a writable folder and open Arconist.exe.
Keep Arconist.pck and data_Arconist.Godot_windows_x86_64 beside the EXE.
No editor, SDK or separate .NET installation is needed.
Requires Windows10/11 x64 and an OpenGL3.3 compatible graphics driver.

Select R004 mixed taps + holds, then click Start chart. Wait3 seconds.
D / F / J / K correspond to the four ground lanes from left to right.
Cyan notes: tap and release at the green line.
Amber bodies: press the lane key when the front meets the line and keep it held.
Green bodies indicate core-confirmed continuous contact activity. Checkpoints
produce feedback/counters; momentary release does not immediately cause a miss.

Mixed chart instructions (time shown by the HUD after the countdown):
  Tap D at2s and K at3s.
  Hold F from4–6.5s. Briefly release and press again during the body.
  Tap K at6s while F is held.
  Hold J from8–11s. Tap D at9.5s using the other hand.
  Tap F at12.5s.
  Hold D from14–16.5s; tap K at15.5s.
  Hold K from18–20s; tap J at21s. Results follow shortly afterward.

Mouse: left-click the lane ground near the green line for taps, or keep the
button pressed to sustain a hold. A held mouse moved to another lane/outside
the track stops sustaining its previous lane on the following frame. Moving
back can restore hold activity; movement does not make a fresh tap judgment.

Two contacts in ONE lane: while J is held, press and HOLD left mouse on J's
ground region near the green line. Release J but keep the mouse pressed.
The hold should continue until both contacts are removed. Try the reverse too.
The short E026 J-hold diagnostic is also selectable. Each new key press/mouse
press has a distinct logical identity; key repeat does not manufacture presses.

Other chart choices retain the original16-tap practice and two-tap reference.
