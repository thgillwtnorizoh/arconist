### Runtime and input

Parsed Hold, HoldState, Latched, Holding, Contacts, activating identities,
CheckpointState.Resolved/Outcome/ResolvedAt and cached GameResult are separate.
DOWN refreshes E but never runs FRAME/range/expiry. Initial hold eligibility is
start<E+100 and E<end; latch writes stop initial iteration unconditionally.
Tap guards and all existing threshold/order behavior remain intact. Distinct-start
chronology is native-supported; source order is the existing engineering tie
break, not a claim about universal equal-start native priority.

Each frame resets activity, traverses all retained contacts and sets activity only
with latch, matching lane and start<E<end. Both contacts may sustain one hold;
neither owns it exclusively. UP removes the actual contact but leaves latch and
current-frame activity until a subsequent frame. A fresh DOWN generates a new
logical identity, including reuse of the same adapter pointer. MOVE only updates
retained lane; lane0 represents a desktop contact outside the supported track.
The mouse membership adapter is a bounded desktop implementation, not Android
transport/capacity/timestamp or physical multitouch equivalence.

### Records, health, score and results

other flag producers are outside scope. Both resolve backwards through the pending

```text

    each retained lane contact activates all eligible latched holds (start<E<end)
```
