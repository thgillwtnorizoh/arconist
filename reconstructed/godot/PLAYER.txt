ARCONIST R003-C01 — Windows x64 / human qualification build

Extract the entire ZIP to a writable folder. Open Arconist.exe.
Keep Arconist.pck and the whole data_Arconist.Godot_windows_x86_64 folder
beside the executable. No Godot editor or separate .NET installation is needed.
Requires Windows 10/11 x64 with an OpenGL 3.3 compatible graphics driver.

Leave “Practice · 16 taps / four lanes” selected and click Start chart.
Wait through the three-second countdown. Tap D, F, J, K for the four lanes
from left to right as notes meet the green judgment line. Release between taps.
Mouse: left-click the ground region of the desired lane near the green line.
Clicks far from the judgment line are rejected. Release between clicks.
After results, click Play again to restart or Back to start to return.
The chart selector also offers the two-tap native reference fixture.

F6: alternate camera. F7: change window size. F8: save an owner-requested
viewport capture under %APPDATA%/Godot/app_userdata/Arconist R003 - 3D Tap Practice/evidence.
Session diagnostics are saved in that same evidence folder.

This is a silent, tap-only research prototype with placeholder graphics.
Holds, arcs, flicks, music, exact original graphics and lane widening are absent.
Losing focus releases held contacts but does not pause chart time.
The build is unsigned. Windows launch, visible rendering, input, results and
restart await the owner's personal inspection and play.
