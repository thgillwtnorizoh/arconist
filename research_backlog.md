## Decision history

- **Scout 001:** proposed all ten gaps and priorities. Arc-tap closure ranked first because its parser, builder and separate judgment evidence support a bounded connection; FlickNote and continuous gauges were explicitly research-gated.
- **Expedition 025 result:** G-001 passes native AFF construction/link/geometry and JNI judgment gates, including an otherwise spatially eligible same-time duplicate reading the native consumed guard. The separate reproducible scenario passes. Evidence: [025 report](excavations/025_native_arc_tap_construction_and_jni_judgment.md). G-002–G-010 statuses are unchanged.
- **Expedition 025 authorization:** headquarters selected G-001 and authorized this backlog plus a minimal reproducible scenario. Other items remain queued/research-gated and are not completed by that selection. The publication report records the final evidence/status of G-001.

Future Scouts may propose reprioritization. Preserve IDs and add decision history rather than silently rewriting the original selection. This backlog does not authorize Expedition 026 or any other task automatically.
